During my tenure last year, I was involved in programs that required me to collaborate with local authorities and colleagues across multiple provinces. I loved getting the opportunity to be exposed to new cultures, languages, and food (ofc). But what I admired most was how close they were to their communities and they knew every corner of their jurisdiction. It was a sense of belonging I hadn't felt in Jakarta since I moved from my hometown. I even "half-jokingly" told them I'd love to work in a regional office someday haha.

Fast forward, it turns out that I got that opportunity. It's been six months since I moved to this city, far away from Java, and I'm grateful for it. Now, I can check off one item from my imaginary checklist that I didn't intend to write haha.

I love how alive this city is (even though I was skeptical at first). The food and cafes are amazing (and much more affordable, suitable for WFC as well). There are over a thousand registered cafes here! And there are so many youths that make this city feel so alive. I guess it's because it hosts the only state uni in this province, thus they all came to study and look for opportunities. I can say this city was like an extended Blok M for sure haha.

Work-wise, the pressure is much more manageable than in Jakarta. I still find myself working after hours sometimes tho, but I've made peace with it. At the end of the day, it's the intensity of the pressure that really makes the difference.

And one more thing: The traffic and the city's size. I can just walk for like less than 3 minutes to the office and the mild traffic makes riding around the city fun. But they don't have a public transport though, which I suppose, makes sense given the size of the city.

But ofc, there are also things that I miss from Jakarta. The old friends, the events, and the opportunity to work with most influential profiles across the country.

Everything changes so fast, and since I likely won't be here for long (due to the regulation changes), I realize I must explore this city more.
